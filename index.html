Forthcoming novella

Dark of Night

Ebook · 15 December 2026

A man may know the trail by day, but darkness can obscure the path

In the high valleys of the Hochkamm, the forest has always been a presence in the lives of those who dwell among its trees. Its paths are known, its dangers respected, and its ancient ways passed from one generation to the next.

But something has changed.

As the first snows of winter approach, an unease settles over an isolated farmstead. The animals grow restless, familiar paths become strange, and the once benign cadences of the forest seem to have taken on a darker meaning.

When fire drives Jakob and his companions from their home, they must find their way through the forest to safety before winter storms close in. But the trails they know no longer lead where they should, and the forest holds dangers they cannot begin to fathom. With each passing hour, the perils around them grow, and the path to safety seems ever more treacherous.

The author

About

M.P. Molenaar writes speculative fiction shaped by an interest in history, folklore and the institutions that shape human societies.

His stories explore the intersections of myth, culture, religion and political power, often through the lives of ordinary people caught up in events beyond their understanding or control.

Drawing on historical traditions and folklore, his work favours richly realised settings, complex human motivations and the quieter mysteries that linger at the edges of the familiar.
